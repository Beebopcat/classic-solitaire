THE CLASSIC RULES

Build four suits.

Move every card to the four foundation piles, starting with an Ace and building up to King in the same suit.

In the seven columns, build downward in alternating colors. A King or a sequence starting with a King can fill an empty column. Any face-up sequence can move together; uncovering a face-down card turns it over.

Click or tap a face-up card, then its destination. You can also drag cards, or double-click a top card to send it to its foundation. Use the stock to draw; when it is empty, tap it to recycle the waste. Redeals are unlimited.

Undo reverses a move. Hint points to a legal move, but does not guarantee a winning deal. Finish game appears when the remaining cards can be safely completed. Some shuffled deals cannot be won.

Keyboard: Tab between cards and controls, Enter or Space to select, Escape to cancel selection, Ctrl+Z to undo.
